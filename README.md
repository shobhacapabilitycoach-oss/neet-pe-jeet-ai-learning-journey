# NEET Pe JEET — AI-Ready Admission Counselling Learning Journey

An L&D portfolio case study demonstrating how a business performance challenge can be translated into a structured admission-counselling capability journey.

## Project at a glance

- **5 connected microlearning modules**
- **30 microlearning lessons**
- Scenario-based practice and reflection
- Facilitator-led learning resources
- Evaluation and reinforcement design
- Rise 360 storyboard and instructional-design rationale
- AI-ready future-state learning architecture

## Learning journey

1. **Build Trust in the First Conversation**
2. **Discover Before You Recommend**
3. **Handle Parent Concerns**
4. **Recommend with Relevance**
5. **The Complete Counselling Conversation**

The journey is designed to move learners from isolated techniques toward an integrated counselling conversation.

## Portfolio resources

The repository includes:

- Interactive browser-based microlearning modules
- AI-Enhanced NEET Pe JEET case study
- Core Facilitator Guide
- Role-Play & Activity Guide
- Evaluation & Reinforcement Guide
- Rise 360 Storyboard & Design Rationale

## Learning-design approach

The experience combines microlearning, scenario practice, reflection, structured conversation frameworks, facilitator support, reinforcement and measurement. The design emphasizes application rather than content consumption alone.

## Evidence and attribution

Historical project evidence is included in the case study. Business movement is presented as **contribution evidence**, not as proof that training alone caused the result; other business and operating factors may also have influenced outcomes.
