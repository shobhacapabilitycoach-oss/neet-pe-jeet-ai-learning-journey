Evaluation plan

Level When Measure Method Owner
1. Reaction End of module Relevance, clarity, confidence to apply 4-item pulse + open comment Facilitator
2. Learning During / end Judgement, discovery, parent response, recommendation quality Scenario checks + scored simulation Facilitator / QA
3. Behaviour Weeks 1–8 Observable counselling behaviours Call review + manager checklist + practice completion Manager / QA
4. Results Monthly Conversion, follow-up quality, cancellations/complaints, learner progression Performance dashboard Business / L&D

Level 1 — Reaction pulse

  • How relevant were the scenarios to real counselling conversations?

  • How clear and usable were the four coaching tools?

  • Which behaviour do you feel more confident applying?

  • What still feels difficult?

Level 2 — Learning checks

  • Trust: Learner chooses an opening that acknowledges before advising.

  • Discovery: Learner follows the previous answer with a relevant probe and confirms the need.

  • Parent concern: Learner demonstrates H.E.A.R. without defensive or high-pressure language.

  • Recommendation: Learner uses Need → Evidence → Benefit and avoids unsupported guarantees.

  • Integrated skill: Learner scores at least 80% on the observer rubric.

Level 3 — Manager observation checklist

  • Creates space before explaining.

  • Asks relevant follow-up questions rather than running a script.

  • Confirms the learner's need before recommending.

  • Explores parent concerns before responding.

  • Uses accurate, relevant evidence rather than feature dumping.

  • Avoids guarantees and pressure.

  • Ends with a clear next step.

30–60 day reinforcement rhythm

Timing Method Output
Within 48 hours First live application Self-reflection
End of Week 1 Peer scenario / micro-practice One adjustment
Week 2 Manager call review Coaching action
Week 3 Retrieval quiz / nudge Framework recall
Week 4 Integrated scenario challenge Behaviour calibration
Weeks 6–8 Trend review + recognition Habit sustainment

AI-enabled reinforcement — future-state design

  • AI Counselling Coach: persona-based student and parent simulations with immediate feedback on observable behaviours.

  • Adaptive practice: assign scenarios based on recurring learner gaps such as weak discovery, premature pitching or parent-concern handling.

  • Manager dashboard: surface practice completion, recurring skill gaps and suggested coaching focus.

  • Conversation reflection assistant: after an approved anonymised interaction, prompt the learner to identify the need, concern, evidence and next step.

  • Governance: use approved content, protect learner/customer data, disclose AI use and require human review for high-stakes feedback.
